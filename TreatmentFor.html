Neuro-Vision Therapy

Neuro-optometric rehabilitation is a specialized treatment approach designed for individuals with visual deficits resulting from various neurological conditions, including traumatic brain injuries, strokes, and other physical disabilities. This therapy aims to improve visual functions and reintegrate visual skills impaired by neurological insults through individualized treatment regimens and exercises.
