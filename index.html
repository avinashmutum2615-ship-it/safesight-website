OUR SERVICES

Computerised Eye Testing

We provide computerised eye testing using high end modern equipments like Matronix Aoturefractometer and verify with manual refraction method for more accurate glass prescription

Comprehensive Eye Examination

During a comprehensive eye test we will check your eyesight and eye health. We screen for eye diseases like glaucoma, macular degeneration, cataracts, melanoma, retinal detachment and much more. We will ask you questions to understand your need and provide optical solutions that will suite your lifestyle.

Contact Lenses (Soft & RGP lens)

Experience personalized vision with our expert fitting of single vision and multifocal saft and hard contact lenses. Choose from our range, including daily, fortnightly, and monthly disposable lenses, ensuring a comfortable and tailored solution for your vision needs.

Myopia Management in Children

Myopia management involves identifying risk factors for myopia (short sightedness) and prescribing appropriate intervention (eye drops, specialised spectacles and contact lenses) to slower down progression of myopia.

Prosthetic Eye Customization

We serve in custom made ocular prosthesis which is commonly known as artificial eye scleral shell, cosmetic shell or a prosthetic eye. We tailor make an eye according to the patient's fellow eye to look normal again with a natural looking eye which can be worn continuously without removing and it moves like an normal eye ball.

Neuro-Vision Therapy

Neuro-optometric rehabilitation is a specialized treatment approach designed for individuals with visual deficits resulting from various neurological conditions, including traumatic brain injuries, strokes, and other physical disabilities. This therapy aims to improve visual functions and reintegrate visual skills impaired by neurological insults through individualized treatment regimens and exercises.

About Us

Safe Sight Eye Care is a well-regarded eye care clinic that focuses on providing high-quality vision services to the patients. The facility offers a wide range of services, including comprehensive eye examinations, diagnosis and treatment of eye conditions, and advanced therapy procedures.

Equipped with the latest technology, Safe Sight Eye Care emphasizes patient comfort and safety, ensuring a thorough and effective approach to eye health. The experienced team of ophthalmologists and optometrists is dedicated to educating patients about preventive care and maintaining optimal vision.

In addition to routine check-ups, they also specialize in treatments for common eye disorders, such as Refractive error, Conjunctivitis, cataracts and many others. Also specialised facilities like Prosthesis, squint diplopia management and neuro vision therapy are available here. With a commitment to community health, Safe Sight Eye Care plays an essential role in promoting eye health awareness and providing accessible care to the local population.
