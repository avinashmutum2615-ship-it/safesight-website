About Us

Safe Sight Eye Care is a well-regarded eye care clinic that focuses on providing high-quality vision services to the patients. The facility offers a wide range of services, including comprehensive eye examinations, diagnosis and treatment of eye conditions, and advanced therapy procedures.

Equipped with the latest technology, Safe Sight Eye Care emphasizes patient comfort and safety, ensuring a thorough and effective approach to eye health. The experienced team of ophthalmologists and optometrists is dedicated to educating patients about preventive care and maintaining optimal vision.

In addition to routine check-ups, they also specialize in treatments for common eye disorders, such as Refractive error, Conjunctivitis, cataracts and many others. Also specialised facilities like Prosthesis, squint diplopia management and neuro vision therapy are available here. With a commitment to community health, Safe Sight Eye Care plays an essential role in promoting eye health awareness and providing accessible care to the local population.
